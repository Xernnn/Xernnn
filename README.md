# Hi, I'm Nguyen Son (Xern)! 👋

I study at the University of Technology Sydney, after Data Science at USTH in Hanoi. These days I mostly build small, local-first tools for my own life: a calendar, a Vietnamese keyboard, and browser extensions that stop feeds from eating my day. Your data stays on your devices, and I use most of these every day.

## 🛠️ What I'm building

| Project | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [xrncal-desktop](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrncal-desktop) | A local-first desktop calendar for Windows and Linux. Google, Microsoft 365 and CalDAV in one window, with Vietnamese lunar dates and lunar-year anniversaries. |
| [xrnikey](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrnikey) | A bilingual Vietnamese–English input method for Linux, on Fcitx5 and IBus. |
| [xrntube](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrntube) | A browser extension that removes blocked YouTube channels and videos from your feed, search and recommendations. |
| [xrnsocial](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrnsocial) | A browser extension that switches off the distracting parts of Facebook, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Twitch. |
| [xrnmoni](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrnmoni) | A self-hosted activity monitor (apps, websites, idle time) for machines you own, with one web dashboard. |
| [xrnhome](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrnhome) | A home inventory app for your phone: what food and things you have, and what needs restocking. |
| [xrnchips](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrnchips) | Poker night without the chips. Stacks, blinds, bets and pots for live Texas Hold'em on one Android phone. |
| [instacover](https://github.com/Xernnn/instacover) | A daily "Day N" Instagram story cover, drawn in Python and sent each morning by a Telegram bot. |

## 🎓 Earlier work
